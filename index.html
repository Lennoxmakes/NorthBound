A little about us

Good coffee doesn't need to be complicated.

Northbound is a neighbourhood coffee shop built around a simple idea: serve coffee we genuinely enjoy and make the space feel just as good as the cup.

We work with carefully selected beans, roast in small batches, and keep the menu focused on things we know we'll be proud to serve. Whether you're here for five minutes or the whole afternoon, you're welcome.
